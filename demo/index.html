Telemed.ai / TeleCred licensure analyst

Run the agent on your own key.

TeleCred reads state medical-board requirements and decides which are clear enough to act on — escalating the ones that aren't, and quoting the exact sentence that made it stop. This page is the agent on its own, without the portal around it.

1 · Your Gemini key

Free from aistudio.google.com/apikey. It is held in a JavaScript variable for as long as this tab is open and never written to storage, never sent to this page's host — this page is static, so it has no server to send anything to. Your key goes from your browser to the agent service that makes the model call, and nowhere else. Close the tab and it's gone.

2 · What the contract requires

Six states are in the frozen dataset. OH is the interesting one — its record contradicts itself, and nothing in the data says so.

3 · What's happening

Capstone prototype for Product Faculty's Agentic AI course. Every record is synthetic — six states, frozen. Nothing on this page is stored: results live in the tab and disappear on refresh. Source: github.com/jalanbyers/schissel-practice-ops
